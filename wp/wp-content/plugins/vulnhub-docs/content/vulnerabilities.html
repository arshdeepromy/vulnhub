The Vulnerabilities screen is the findings list — one row per open (or closed) finding. It is where every dashboard drill-down lands, and every filter it offers is expressible in the URL, so a link can carry an exact query. This page explains what each filter selects.

Findings, not vulnerabilities. Despite the name, each row is a finding — one vulnerability on one asset. The same vulnerability appearing on 40 machines is 40 rows. Filter by a vulnerability (vuln_id) to see all its findings at once.

The default view

Out of the box the list shows reportable, open findings — live findings (open or reopened), not excepted, on in-scope assets (see lifecycle scoping). That is the set a reported number is about. Widen it with the lifecycle and state controls below.

The filters

State & lifecycle

StateOpen (the default, = open + reopened), or a specific state. Dashboard links pass state=open_any.
Lifecycle scopeReportable (default), In service (all), All, or a specific lifecycle status — how much of the estate to include.
ExceptedWhether to include, exclude or show only findings covered by an approved exception.
OverdueOnly findings past their SLA due date. (The dashboard's "Past SLA" tile links here.)

Severity & age

SeverityOne or more of Critical / High / Medium / Low / Info. There is also an "exclude" form (used by the Downloads widget to say "not info").
AgeBy how long a finding has been open: new (under 30 days), mid (30–90), old (over 90). The severity-and-age matrix links here.
Patch availableWhether the vendor has published a fix. "No patch available" is the set that needs a decision rather than a patch window.

Attack path (from the threat widget)

Routeedge, user or inside — see Routes, delivery & reachability. The edge filter additionally restricts to internet-facing assets; inside also folds in edge findings on unreachable machines.
DeliveryWithin the user route: email, website or download.
Exploit (poc)poc=1 restricts to findings with a working exploit — what the attack-path widget counts.
Zone / platformInstall-path zone (e.g. downloads) and OS platform (windows/linux/macos) — used by the Downloads-folder widget.

Where & who

ProductA software product slug — every finding for that product (the Exposure by product drill-down).
Vulnerabilityvuln_id — all findings of one scanner definition (the "Most widespread" drill-down).
TeamFindings on assets owned by a team (the Exposure by team drill-down).
DepartmentFindings on devices owned by people in a department (from the Entra enrichment — see Departments). The Vulnerabilities by department widget drills here, and Export CSV carries it.
Asset typeWorkstation / server / mobile / network / cloud / appliance.
SearchFree-text across the finding, its vulnerability and its asset.

Reading a row

Each row shows the finding's severity, the vulnerability title, the asset it is on, its state, age and risk score, and any attached ticket or exception. Opening a finding shows the scanner's output (the evidence), the solution, the CVEs and exploit evidence, and the actions available to you — raise a ticket or request an exception (subject to your role). See Tickets & exceptions.

Filters compose, and they live in the URL. Because every filter is a query parameter, you can bookmark or share a precise view — "open, exploitable, edge-route findings on servers" is just a link. That is exactly how the dashboard hands you a pre-filtered list when you click a number.
