Everything in VulnHub lives in one portal. The top bar carries the primary navigation on the left, and — for administrators — an Administration link and your account menu on the right, along with a light/dark theme toggle. This page walks through each destination.

The primary navigation

DashboardThe widget board — the at-a-glance picture of exposure, coverage, ownership and remediation. Fully covered in The dashboard, widget by widget.
VulnerabilitiesThe findings list: every open (or closed) finding, with a rich set of filters. This is where dashboard drill-downs land. See The Vulnerabilities list.
AssetsThe asset register: every machine, its owner, coverage state, reachability and findings. See Assets & owners.
TicketsRemediation tickets raised in Jira, their status, and the verification result when they close. See Tickets & exceptions.
ExceptionsThe risk-acceptance register: formally accepted risks, with reasons, approvers and expiry dates.
VendorsExposure grouped by vendor.
DocsThis handbook and developer wiki.

Products (exposure grouped by software product) is reached from the "View all products" control on the dashboard's Exposure by product widget rather than from the main nav. If the advisory-watch plugin is active, an Alerts destination appears too, sometimes with a badge counting new advisories that match your estate.

The Administration area

Administrators reach the admin area from the Administration link in the top bar (it only appears if you hold the manage capability). Its sections are grouped into Data, Integrations and Platform:

Admin overviewConnector health, data freshness, and what needs attention.
IntegrationsConfigure Tenable, Intune, Jira, CMDB and single sign-on.
ImportsUpload large CMDB and Tenable CSV exports (resumable, up to very large files).
RulesThe asset-classification rules — categorise and prioritise assets and decide who owns what. Also where the exposure rule (observed / asserted) is set.
Teams & SLAsRemediation owners, Jira routing and per-severity response targets.
Sync activityEvery connector run, with its full log. Start here when data looks stale.
Audit trailWho changed what, and when.
SettingsPlatform behaviour, ownership policy, retention, and thresholds (e.g. the EPSS threshold).
DocumentationA link straight to this wiki.

Integration plugins (Jira, Backup, and others) add their own sections here automatically. Which sections you see depends on your role — see the Portal framework page for how access is gated.

Who can see what

Access is capability-based. In short: Viewers can read the portal; Analysts can also triage, raise tickets, request exceptions and run syncs; Approvers can approve exceptions; Administrators can do everything, including configure connectors. A "portal-only" user is one who can use the portal but is kept out of the WordPress admin entirely. The specifics are in Portal, views & navigation.

Getting around fast. Almost every number on the dashboard is a link into the Vulnerabilities or Assets list with the right filters already applied — so the quickest way to "the list behind this figure" is to click the figure, not to rebuild the filter by hand.
